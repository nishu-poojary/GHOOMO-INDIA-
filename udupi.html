Udupi

Temple Town of Karnataka

Udupi is a beautiful coastal town in Karnataka, famous for the Sri Krishna Temple, pristine beaches, delicious Udupi cuisine, and scenic landscapes. It is a great destination for exploring spirituality, culture, nature, and coastal beauty.
