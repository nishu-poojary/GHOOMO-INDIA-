Mangalore

Rome of the East

Mangalore, also known as 'Mangaluru', is a beautiful coastal city in Karnataka famous for its pristine beaches, historic temples, churches, and rich culture. It is known for attractions like Panambur Beach, Tannirbhavi Beach, Kadri Manjunatha Temple, St. Aloysius Chapel, and Sultan Battery. With delicious coastal cuisine and scenic surroundings, Mangalore is a great destination for relaxation, culture, and adventure.
